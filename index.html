October Movie Challenge
Prediction Draw — Official Rules

No purchase necessary · Entry is free · Void where prohibited by law

  • Entry: YouTube comments only
  • Window: before 5:00 PM CST daily
  • Period: Oct 1–31, 2026
  • Prize: movie theatre gift card

Guess the pumpkin rating (1–5) on that day's video before 5:00 PM CST. Closest guess earns one entry, an exact match earns two. Entries stack up all month toward one prize draw.

Eligibility

Open to legal residents of Canada (excluding Quebec) and the continental United States, aged 18 or older. Employees of Mr Alixander: Watches (i.e., Alix) and their immediate household are not eligible.

How to Enter

  1. Comment your prediction of the daily pumpkin score (1–5) on that day's video on YouTube before 5:00 PM CST. Predictions submitted after 5:00 PM CST that day, once the actual rating has been posted, will not be counted.
  2. Like and subscribe to the channel (free, required for eligibility, does not affect odds of winning).

YouTube Is the Only Official Entry Platform

This series is also cross-posted to TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook, but those are for watching and following along only. Predictions left as comments on TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook are not eligible and will not be counted, even if correct. To enter, your prediction must be a comment on the YouTube video itself.

Earning Entries

Each day, the commenter(s) with the closest prediction to the actual score earn one entry into the final prize draw. An exact match earns two entries instead of one. Entries accumulate across the full month, so the more days you play, the better your odds.

One entry per person, per day. Only your first valid prediction on each day's video counts. Every guess you post after your first is excluded, it will not be used to determine closeness or eligibility, and it does not improve your odds that day. Posting multiple guesses to try to land on the exact score will not work.

Contest Period

October 1, 2026 through October 31, 2026, 5:00 PM CST each day for that day's entry window.

My review and rating post after 5:00 PM CST daily — the prediction window for that day closes for good once it does. There is no retroactive entry for a missed day.

Winner Selection

At the end of the month, all accumulated entries are pooled and one winner is selected at random from that pool. A skill-testing question will be administered to the selected winner before the prize is awarded, as required by Canadian law.

Prize

Prize

One (1) gift card to a movie theatre chain (e.g., Cineplex), approximate retail value TBD. The specific chain will be selected based on what is available in the winner's region. If no movie theatre chain operates in the winner's area, no cash or alternate prize will be substituted. Prize is non-transferable.

How Entries Are Tracked

Winning predictions are identified and logged once daily, shortly after the 5:00 PM CST cutoff. Only clear, numeric predictions in top-level comments are counted; unclear, sarcastic, or ambiguous guesses may be excluded at Alix's discretion. If comments are disabled or a video is unavailable for any reason, that day is voided with no entries awarded, and no makeup day will be added.

Winner Notification

The winner will be announced in the Halloween finale video and contacted via YouTube comment or channel message. If no response is received within 7 days, an alternate winner will be selected.
