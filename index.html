Update posted October 2, 2026

The October Movie Challenge and its Prediction Draw are not running this year.

Why

I've got a lot on the go across my channels right now, and reviewing a horror movie every single day of October takes more energy than I can give it while still doing it well. Rather than half-do it, I'm pressing pause.

What this means

  • No daily horror reviews this October
  • The pumpkin score prediction contest is cancelled. No entries are being collected and no prize will be drawn.

Will it come back?

That's the plan. This challenge has been a five-year tradition and I'd love to bring it back in some form next October. Subscribe to Mr Alixander: Watches so you'll know when it does.

Thanks for understanding, and happy Halloween.
